## Map Knowledge information

### What is this page?

Here, you can explore the different maps available in Rainbow Six Siege and learn what makes each one unique. Every map has its own layout, rooms, entry points, and strategies that can affect how a match plays out. Whether you're attacking or defending, understanding the map can give you an advantage over your opponents.

This section provides information about the maps, including their layouts to help players improve their map knowledge. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced player, learning the maps is an important part of becoming better at Rainbow Six Siege.

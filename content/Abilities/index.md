## Operators Abilities 

### Description

Abilities are an important part of Rainbow Six Siege because they make each operator unique and give players different ways to approach a match. Every operator has a special ability or gadget that can help their team gather information, defend objectives, create openings, or gain an advantage over opponents. Understanding how these abilities work can make a big difference in your overall gameplay.

This section covers the different abilities available to **Attackers and Defenders**, including how they work, their strengths and weaknesses, and the best situations to use them. You'll also learn how certain abilities work together and how to counter enemy operators. Whether you're new to the game or looking to improve your skills, learning about operator abilities can help you make better decisions, develop stronger strategies, and become a more effective teammate.

## Gadget Information

### What is this page

Gadgets are an important part of Rainbow Six Siege because they allow operators to gather information, protect their teammates, create openings, and stop enemy attacks. Each gadget has a different purpose, and knowing when and how to use it can make a big difference during a match.

This section covers the different types of gadgets available to Attackers and Defenders, including drones, breaching tools, explosives, traps, and defensive equipment. You'll learn what each gadget does, how it works, and how it can be used effectively during gameplay. Whether you're learning the basics or trying to improve your strategies, understanding gadgets can help you become a better teammate and make smarter decisions during matches.

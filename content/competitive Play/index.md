## Competitive Play Information

### What is this page

Competitive play is where players put their skills, teamwork, communication, and game knowledge to the test. Unlike casual matches, competitive matches require players to think carefully about their strategies, operator selections, and how they work together as a team.

This section focuses on the competitive side of Rainbow Six Siege, including Ranked matches, the ranking system, team strategies, and ways to improve your performance. You'll learn how competitive matches work, why communication is important, and what it takes to climb through the ranks. Whether you're just starting your Ranked journey or trying to reach a higher rank, this section will help you understand competitive gameplay and develop better habits as a player.

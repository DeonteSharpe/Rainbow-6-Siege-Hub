# What is Teamwork

Teamwork is one of the most important parts of playing Rainbow Six Siege, especially in Ranked Mode. Unlike some shooting games where players can focus mainly on getting eliminations, Rainbow Six Siege requires players to communicate, plan strategies, and work together to complete objectives.

Even if one player has excellent aiming skills, winning can still be difficult when the team does not communicate or support each other.

## Communication During Matches

Communication helps players share important information about enemy locations, traps, and objectives. Players can communicate through voice chat, text chat, or the game's ping system.

For example, if a defender notices an attacker entering through a window, they can alert their teammates. This gives everyone a better chance to prepare instead of being surprised.

### Ways to Improve Teamwork

Players can improve their teamwork by following these tips:

- Give clear callouts about enemy locations.
- Use cameras and drones to gather information.
- Choose [[Operator Guied/index|operators]] that support the team's strategy.
- Help teammates defend or attack objectives.
- Avoid blaming teammates when mistakes happen.
- Stay focused on winning rounds instead of only getting eliminations.

## Choosing Operators That Work Together

Operator selection is another important part of teamwork. Each operator has unique gadgets and abilities that can help the team.

For example, Thermite can open reinforced walls when the necessary conditions are met, while Thatcher can help disable certain defender electronics. Using these operators together can make attacking an objective easier.

Defenders can also combine gadgets to slow down attackers and protect important areas.

> A team that communicates and works together can often perform better than a group of skilled players who refuse to cooperate.

Overall, teamwork is a major part of success in Ranked Mode. Players who support each other, share information, and communicate clearly have a better chance of winning matches and [[Tips for Improving Your Rank in Rainbow Six Siege|improving]] their ranks.

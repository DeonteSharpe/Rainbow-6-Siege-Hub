# How it works

Rainbow Six Siege has a ranking system that allows players to compete against others and track their progress. The purpose of the ranking system is to give players something to work toward while encouraging them to improve their skills. Players can move through different ranks by participating in competitive matches and earning Ranked Points (RP).

## Understanding Ranked Points

Ranked Points determine a player's visible rank in Rainbow Six Siege. When players win Ranked matches, they earn RP, which helps them move closer to the next division. Losing matches can cause players to lose RP, making it harder to reach higher ranks.

The game also uses a skill rating system to help match players against opponents with similar competitive abilities. This rating is different from the visible rank that players see on their profiles.

### Why the Ranking System Matters

The ranking system gives players a way to measure their competitive progress. However, having a higher rank does not automatically mean someone is better at every part of the game.

Some players are excellent at aiming, while others are better at gathering information, defending objectives, or supporting teammates.

**Important things to understand about Ranked:**

- Winning matches helps players earn RP.
- Losing matches can decrease RP.
- Matchmaking considers player skill.
- Players progress through different rank divisions.
- [[The Importance of Teamwork in Rainbow Six Siege Ranked|Teamwork]] and communication can improve match results.

> Your rank shows your competitive progress, but improving your skills is what makes you a better player.

Overall, the ranking system encourages players to continue [[Understanding Ranked Mode|learning]], practicing, and [[Tips for Improving Your Rank in Rainbow Six Siege|improving]] their performance.

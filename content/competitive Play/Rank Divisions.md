# What it is

Rainbow Six Siege has several competitive ranks that players can work toward while playing Ranked Mode. These ranks represent different stages of progression and help players understand where they currently stand in the competitive system.

The ranking system begins with Copper and continues through Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, and Champion. Each rank has its own appearance and represents a different level of achievement.

## Understanding the Different Ranks

The main competitive ranks in Rainbow Six Siege are:

1. **Copper** – The starting rank in the visible progression system.
2. **Bronze** – The next step after progressing through Copper.
3. **Silver** – A middle stage of early ranked progression.
4. **Gold** – A rank that players reach after progressing beyond Silver.
5. **Platinum** – A higher competitive division.
6. **Emerald** – A division between Platinum and Diamond.
7. **Diamond** – One of the highest competitive ranks.
8. **Champion** – The highest competitive rank in Rainbow Six Siege.

Most ranks have five smaller divisions. Players move through these divisions by [[How the Ranking system Works|earning Ranked Points]] from winning matches.

## Why Rank Divisions Are Important

Rank divisions give players smaller goals to work toward instead of focusing only on reaching Champion. For example, someone in Silver might focus on reaching Gold before thinking about Platinum.

Players should also understand that a rank does not always represent every skill they have. Some players may be great at communication but struggle with aiming, while others may be skilled at winning gunfights but have difficulty working with [[The Importance of Teamwork in Rainbow Six Siege Ranked|teamates]].

Overall, rank divisions make competitive progression easier to follow and give players a reason to continue improving throughout the season.

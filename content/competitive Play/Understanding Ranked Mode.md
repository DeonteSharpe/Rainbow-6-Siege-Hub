# Understanding Ranked Mode in Rainbow Six Siege

Ranked Mode is one of the main competitive experiences in Rainbow Six Siege. It allows players to compete against other teams while working toward improving their competitive rank. Compared to more casual game modes, Ranked places a greater focus on teamwork, strategy, and understanding the game's mechanics.

Players must work together to attack or defend objectives while making decisions that can affect the outcome of each round.

## How Ranked Matches Work

Ranked matches use the Bomb game mode, where two teams compete as attackers and defenders. The attacking team must locate the bomb sites and attempt to plant the defuser or eliminate the defending team.

The defending team must protect the bomb sites, prevent the defuser from being planted, or disable it if the attackers successfully plant it.

Players switch between attacking and defending during the match, which means they need to understand both sides of the game.

### Preparing for Ranked Mode

Before playing Ranked, players should understand several important things:

- Learn the basic abilities of attacking and defending operators.
- Understand how to reinforce walls and protect objectives.
- Practice using drones and security cameras.
- Learn the layouts of frequently played maps.
- Communicate with teammates during matches.
- Understand how the defuser works in Bomb mode.

## Why Players Enjoy Ranked

Many players enjoy Ranked because it provides a more challenging experience than casual matches. Every victory helps players work toward their competitive goals, while losses provide opportunities to learn from mistakes.

Ranked also encourages players to develop better strategies and become familiar with different operators.

For example, attackers may need to coordinate their gadgets to enter a defended room, while defenders must work together to protect important areas.

> Ranked Mode is not just about winning gunfights. It is about using teamwork, strategy, and information to complete the objective.

Overall, Ranked Mode gives Rainbow Six Siege players an opportunity to test their skills, improve their gameplay, and compete against other players.

## Operator Guide Information

### What is this page?

Here, you can learn about the different operators available in Rainbow Six Siege and what makes each one unique. Every operator has their own weapons, special gadgets, and abilities that can help their team during a match. Whether you prefer attacking or defending, choosing the right operator can make a big difference in how your team performs.

This section provides information about both Attackers and Defenders, including their roles, abilities, weapons, and useful strategies. You'll also learn how different operators work together and how their gadgets can help your team gain an advantage. Whether you're new to the game or looking to improve your skills, this guide will help you better understand each operator and find the ones that fit your playstyle.

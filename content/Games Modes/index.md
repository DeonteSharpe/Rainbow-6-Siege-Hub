## Game mode Information

### What is this page

Rainbow Six Siege offers different game modes that allow players to experience the game in different ways. Some modes focus on teamwork and competitive strategies, while others offer faster matches and more casual gameplay. Each mode has its own rules, objectives, and challenges that can change how you approach a match.

This section covers the current game modes in Rainbow Six Siege, including **Quick Match, Unranked, Ranked, Arcade, Training, and Custom Games**. You'll learn how these modes work, what makes them different, and which ones might fit your playstyle. Whether you're practicing your skills, playing with friends, or competing to reach a higher rank, understanding the different game modes can help you get the most out of Rainbow Six Siege.
